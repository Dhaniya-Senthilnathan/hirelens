Why hirelens

Built for the twenty-tabs-open job search.

Most engineering students apply to a role, wait two weeks, and get a rejection email with no explanation. hirelens exists so you find out where you stand before you hit submit — not after.

01The problem

Job postings list fifteen required skills. Your resume lists twelve, worded differently, buried under project descriptions. Nobody actually checks the overlap by hand — you just apply and hope. That guesswork is what this tool replaces.

02How it works

Upload a resume and paste a job description. Both get parsed against a curated dictionary of 500+ technical terms — languages, frameworks, cloud tools, security concepts — matched through phrase matching with spaCy's NER as a fallback for anything the dictionary misses.

The match score itself isn't a simple overlap count. It's built from TF‑IDF vectors of both documents compared with cosine similarity, so a skill central to the job posting counts for more than one mentioned once in passing.

03Who it's for

Engineering students and early-career developers applying to their first few roles — where the gap between "I know this" and "I can prove it on paper" is usually the entire problem.
